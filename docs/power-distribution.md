# Power Distribution

The project report shows:

- A 12V DC supply for selected motor and pump loads.
- A 5V DC supply for the NodeMCU/controller side.
- LM317 regulator stages in the illustrated circuit.

Before practical operation, verify the regulator output, load current, grounding, polarity, and the voltage rating of every actuator. Do not power motors or pumps directly from an ESP8266 GPIO.
